Privacy Policy

Nap Pile

1. Who we are and what this covers

This policy describes how Daiki Hirose (“we”, “us”) handles your information in the app Nap Pile (Japanese name おひるねのやま, the “App”). The App is a puzzle game in which you stack sleepy animals on a bed to merge them.

2. Information stored on your device

The App has no account registration. The following information is stored on your device. Information used for analytics, purchases and advertising consent is also processed through external services as described in Sections 3 and 4:

  • Game progress (scores, best records, daily streak, the collection, how many games you played today)
  • Your in-game wallet (Shard balance, tools, purchased or unlocked themes and music)
  • Settings (sound, haptics, language, theme, reminder time, etc.)
  • Your advertising consent choices (Section 4)

A copy of the wallet is also kept in the device keychain so it can survive reinstalling the App on the same device. The App does not sync the entire wallet to an external service. Wallet information sent for analytics and purchase and grant records are described in Section 3.

The daily reminder is a local notification created on your device and is off by default. No push notification server is used to deliver it. When analytics is enabled, information about notification permission, settings, time (hour only) and launches from reminders is sent as described in Section 3.

3. Information processed through third-party services

The App uses the services below. Each provider processes the information described below and connection information such as IP addresses under its own privacy policy.

  • Apple Inc.: payment processing through the App Store. We do not receive payment details such as card numbers; Apple handles them.
  • RevenueCat, Inc.: verifying and restoring in-app purchases. Processes an anonymous app user ID, transaction and receipt data, purchase status and technical information such as device and OS details, and maintains records of Shards granted through purchases. This does not sync the entire wallet, including Shards earned through play or spent in the App. To aggregate advertising revenue, it also receives ad events (loading, display, clicks and load failures, placement, format, ad unit ID, impression ID, revenue amount, currency and revenue precision) tied to the same anonymous ID.
  • Unity Technologies SF (Unity LevelPlay / Unity Ads): serving and measuring ads. Through its mediation, ads from Unity Ads and from advertising partners participating in its ad exchange may be shown. Processes the advertising identifier, ad impression and click information, and device information. See Section 4.
  • InMobi Pte. Ltd. (InMobi CMP / Choice): collecting and recording advertising consent for users in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, and providing privacy settings for US state laws. Stores the consent state on your device under the IAB Transparency & Consent Framework (TCF) and passes it to advertising providers. US choices use GPP. The SDK also communicates to retrieve configuration and support consent management.
  • PostHog Inc.: usage analytics and troubleshooting to improve the game. The following information is sent with a randomly generated per-device identifier and session identifiers:
    • App launches and updates, moves to the foreground or background, first launch date, launches from reminders, and tutorial starts, progress, exits, completion and rewards
    • Play counts, scores and best records, game duration and reason for ending, drops, merges, highest tier, bears born and vanished, chains, Shards earned and their breakdown, tool use and stock, revive methods, new records, discoveries and daily streak
    • Reaching the play limit and the action chosen, where a pass offer was opened, products purchased, purchase attempts and results, restore results, shop tabs viewed, and tools, themes or music bought with Shards, their price and the balance after purchase
    • Pass status and plan, selected themes and music, music previews, number of themes owned and Shard balance (rounded to the nearest 100 in ongoing status updates; purchases made with Shards include the exact balance after purchase)
    • Changes to sound, volume, haptics, guide line, language and analytics settings; tutorial completion; notification permission, settings and time (hour only); where sharing was initiated and its result; collection views and discovery counts; and configuration loading results
    • Ad requests, results and placements, where consent screens were opened, whether GDPR applies and whether a response has been recorded (the App does not send consent strings to PostHog)
    • App and SDK names and versions, build number, device model, manufacturer and type, OS and version, screen size, whether the device is an emulator, and event and sending times and identifiers. SDK launch events may include the URL that opened the App
    • Diagnostic information about unhandled JavaScript errors and rejected promises, including error type, message, severity, location and call history (stack traces)

    Analytics does not ask you to enter your name, email address or phone number, and the App does not link those details or the advertising identifier to the random identifier. It does not collect precise location such as GPS, and PostHog’s region estimation from IP addresses is disabled. Connection information such as IP addresses is processed when communicating with the service.

    You can stop new usage and error records in Settings under “Share usage data” (on by default). Information recorded before you turned it off may still be sent, and communication to retrieve SDK configuration may still occur. Turning it off does not delete information already sent (Section 6).

  • Cloudflare, Inc.: delivering a configuration file we operate. Your IP address and similar connection data are processed transiently when it is fetched.

4. Advertising and consent

  1. Ads you will see: video ads you choose to watch by tapping a button (revive, extra plays, double shards, shop) and at most one full-screen ad per day. Full-screen ads never appear during play and are not shown to Nap Pass subscribers. Stopping a video ad early has no penalty.
  2. iOS tracking permission (ATT): right before the first ad, iOS asks whether the App may use the advertising identifier (IDFA). If you allow it, advertising providers may use the identifier to show ads matched to your interests. If you decline, the identifier is not collected and you only see ads that are not based on your interests. Your choice does not change what you can play, and you can change it at any time in iOS Settings.
  3. Users in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland: before the first ad, InMobi CMP (Choice) shows a consent screen asking for your consent to the use of information for advertising. You can change your choices at any time from “Privacy settings” in the App’s settings.
  4. Users in certain US states: where state law applies, “Privacy settings” in the App’s settings lets you opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information. We ourselves never sell or share your personal information.
  5. Ad content: the ad content rating is set to be suitable for all audiences.

5. Information we do not collect

The App does not ask you to register your name, email address or phone number and has no feature that reads your contacts, photos, health data or browsing history in other apps or websites. If you contact us, we receive your email address and the content of your request. The App does not collect precise location such as GPS. Advertising providers may, however, infer approximate location such as your country or region from your IP address (Sections 3 and 4). Usage analytics and crash reports go through PostHog (Section 3); these include usage, device information and error messages associated with a random identifier. You can stop new records in Settings. There is no account registration, cloud sync of the entire progress and wallet, or push notification.

6. Retention and deletion

Ordinary App storage is removed when you delete the App, but the wallet copy in the keychain may remain after reinstalling on the same device. Deleting the App therefore does not erase all information. The App has no feature that syncs the entire progress and wallet to another device. Purchase and grant records and analytics already sent are retained by external services separately from local storage. If you would like the purchase records tied to your anonymous RevenueCat ID, or the usage data tied to your random PostHog identifier, deleted, contact us (Section 10) and we will request the deletion. Information processed by Apple, Unity, InMobi, RevenueCat, Cloudflare and PostHog is governed by their policies and applicable law.

7. Your rights

The App is available worldwide. If you are in the EEA, the UK or another jurisdiction with similar laws, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to the processing of your information and to data portability. You can exercise these rights through the contact in Section 10. Our legal bases are: performance of a contract for purchases; your consent for interest-based advertising; our legitimate interests for fraud prevention and for basic ad delivery where permitted; and our legitimate interests for usage analytics (which you can turn off at any time in Settings). You can withdraw consent at any time (Section 4). You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the supervisory authority in your country or region.

International transfers: Apple, RevenueCat, Unity, InMobi, Cloudflare and PostHog may process information outside your country, including in the United States and Japan. We rely on the safeguards each provider maintains.

8. Children

The App is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you are a parent or guardian and believe we have collected information from your child, please contact us (Section 10).

9. Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be announced on this page or by other appropriate means.
